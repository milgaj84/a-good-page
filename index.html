Shortcuts and tips

Your Library: everything you write is kept in one folder and saves by itself. The Library holds projects; a project holds one or many pages that you can drag into order. Pages outside any project sit under Unfiled pages.

Go anywhere: press Ctrl/Cmd+P and type. It finds pages and every command.

Formatting: select text for a quick bar, or type / on an empty line. Markdown shortcuts such as # and - also work.

Quiet screen: the bars fade while you type. Move the mouse or press Esc to bring them back.

Safety: if a file changes outside the app, saving pauses and you choose what to keep. History keeps earlier versions of every page.

Things you moved to the trash wait here. Restore puts them back where they were; nothing is erased.

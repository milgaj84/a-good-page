Shortcuts

Write freely. The page stores Markdown, but you format it like a document.

Working directory: Click Workspace, choose a folder, browse its subfolders and open .md, .markdown or .txt files. The five most recently selected folders stay at the top for one-click switching. Use breadcrumbs or ↑ for parent folders; filter the visible folder by name (↓ moves into the results, Enter opens a sole result, Esc clears the filter first), and ↻ to refresh. Remove a recent shortcut with × (files stay on disk). Choosing a folder alone does not close your current document; opening a file follows the usual unsaved-work check. Save As starts in the folder you are viewing.

Before exporting: Export PDF shows actual paginated pages. Choose Manuscript for spacious pages or Reading copy for a compact shareable layout, then export what you see.

Editing in another app: If your open file changes on disk, autosave pauses and a conflict dialog offers Review changes, Reload from disk, Save my version as a copy, or Keep writing. Keep safety snapshot & reload first keeps the current draft in Time Machine and then replaces the in-memory page; Save a copy leaves the outside edit alone. A missing or unreadable file cannot be reloaded. The dialog names the file and distinguishes an occupied copy filename. Already queued autosaves stop on conflict; an explicit Save rechecks.

While saving or exporting: New edits remain unsaved until another save. A PDF uses the previewed snapshot; if you edit during its save dialog, those later words are not in that PDF. Cancelled exports with newer edits require a fresh preview.

First time here? A three-step guide offers to create, save and export your first page. Skip it whenever you like; Show writing guide below reopens it.

Quiet screen: Once you start typing, the top bar, the status bar and the scrollbar fade away. Move the mouse or press Esc to bring them back. Turn this off in Aa or with Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+J.

Typewriter line: Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+T (or Aa) holds the line you are typing at the middle of the window. Clicking never makes the page jump.

Zen draft: For a first draft that keeps moving. Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+D pauses Backspace, Delete, Cut and dragging text away, so you write now and edit later. Undo, typing and arrow keys still work. A “Zen draft” tag sits in the status bar; click it or press the shortcut again to edit normally. Zen draft is off whenever the app starts.

Focus choices: Use the arrow by Focus for Paragraph, Sentence or Full screen. Sentence dims surrounding words; Full screen hides the app chrome. Esc returns to normal.

Quick formatting: Type / at the start of an empty paragraph to choose a block in everyday language. Selecting text opens a floating menu with Title, Heading and Scene break.

Find and replace: Click Find or press Ctrl/Cmd+F. Selecting a short phrase first fills the search field. Toggle Match case or Whole words, see live match counts, navigate with Enter / Shift+Enter, and press Enter in Replace with to replace the current match. Ctrl/Cmd+H opens replacement.

Chapters: Open Outline; the arrows jump to adjacent top-level chapters and triangles collapse subheadings. When quitting with unsaved words, choose save, discard, or keep writing.

Long projects: Ctrl/Cmd+P jumps to any chapter or workspace file by a few letters. Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+R pins a notes file beside the page, read-only; collapse it to a tab or move it to the other side. Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+A sets a sprint goal for new words; the corner ring glows once when you reach it. Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+I opens Time Machine: drag the slider through earlier versions, Restore one (Undo reverses it), or Export recovery copy to a separate file without changing the manuscript. Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Q polishes dashes, ellipses and quotes, leaving code alone.

Find anything: Click Commands or press Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+P. Type a word, use ↑/↓ and Enter. Try “heading”, “PDF” or “session”.

Writing session: Click Session, choose minutes and optionally a target for new words. The page enters focus mode, a quiet timer stays visible, and a gentle summary appears when time runs out. Reopen Session to finish early.

Format: Use the top bar for headings, emphasis, lists, links and scene breaks. Select words for quick tools. You can also type #, ##, >, -, [ ], **bold**, *italic* or --- followed by a space.

Files: Open, drop or save .md, .markdown and .txt files. Plain text opens literally and saves without Markdown styling. Export PDF creates a separate paginated copy; it does not replace your draft.

Your space: Choose Paper, Sepia, Sage, Night or Midnight with Theme. Aa changes type, size, column width, spacing and your word goal. Outline jumps between headings. Named files autosave; untitled drafts are kept locally. Themes cross-fade softly, and animations stay still when your system asks to reduce motion.

Typography: In Aa, Column width keeps lines at about 60 (Narrow), 72 (Comfortable) or 85 (Wide) characters, whatever the window size. Typeface offers an editorial serif for narrative, a humanist sans for articles and notes, and Duospace for drafting. Spacing sets line height and the gap between paragraphs: Dense, Balanced or Spacious. Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+M, K and Y switch width, spacing and typeface. Fonts such as Literata, Inter or iA Writer Duo are used if installed; nothing is downloaded. PDF export keeps its own layout.

Fast scrolling: Click the mouse wheel on the page, then move the pointer up or down: the further you go, the faster it scrolls. Click again, press any key or turn the wheel to stop. Or hold the wheel, drag and release.
